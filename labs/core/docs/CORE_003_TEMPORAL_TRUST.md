# KEP-CORE-003: Causal synthetic source-quality learning and abstention

Status: RESEARCH ONLY. Related PR #38, ADR-0027, and CORE-001/002.

## Change in development direction

No new real data search, public website crawling, Parquet/registry acquisition, human field review or new model. Keep every previous raw and pinned Istanbul archive and FIELD-001 private study untouched. Build the synthetic KEP decision kernel instead.

CORE-002 made a concrete laboratory mistake visible: when hypothetical source sensitivity/specificity dropped, the VOI policy sometimes lost normalized utility versus never checking. CORE-003 therefore replaces *fixed assumed source error rates* with rates measured from historical, independently audited, entirely fictional sensors. The output remains a research check proposal, not an empirical business probability.

## System contract: time, sample independence and roots

Every immutable AuditFeedback includes a source event ID, fictional case ID, publisher root, source observation time, time its independent label became accessible to KEP, whether the publisher supported the claim, an *independent simulator ground-truth label*, and explicitly restricted synthetic provenance. The current case's true label is **never** sent as a historical audit, and test-case truth/prospective sensor replies remain sealed from the planning input.

- Both timestamps must be timezone aware and causal. A label not yet adjudicated at the query cutoff is NOT available to the planner even if its future value is pre-generated by the simulator.
- Audits must have sampling=independent_uniform. Policy-selected/opportunistic or unknown-origin data are rejected, not treated as unbiased. This models a capability requiring a separate future audit program, not actual human independence.
- (publisher root, fictional case ID) contributes at most one current observation. Equal-time contradictory copies are rejected; identical retry is idempotent. Publisher copies do not increase sample size.
- A bounded rolling **72-hour** window in the fixed CORE-003 simulator limits stale quality evidence. At least **12 independent truth-positive** and **12 truth-negative** examples per source root are needed before it is called READY.
- Class-conditional sensitivity and specificity are separately estimated using symmetric Beta(2,2) smoothing. Wilson nominal 95% lower scores represent uncertainty for these *assumed independent synthetic audits*, not universal real-world risk guarantees.
- Current missing audit evidence -> NO_CHECK_INSUFFICIENT_AUDITS. A conservative policy that sees a lower sensitivity or specificity below **0.62** in the source supplying the first positive root returns NO_CHECK_UNCERTAIN_ORIGIN; weak hypothetical prospective roots are excluded.
- The optional posterior-mean comparator only uses smoothed central rates. The frozen-VOI comparator retains CORE-002's original authored rates. NEVER and CHEAPEST provide further controls.
- All calibrated hypothetical rates are passed into the EXISTING CORE-002 one-check decision planner. No alternate booking, recommendation, source acquisition, authorization or model-calling engine is introduced.

The root identities are modeled as independent for fusion, but the simulator includes a special regime where different publisher roots secretly share a bad rumor. Marginal sensitivity/specificity alone cannot prove real independence and may fail on correlated errors.

## Frozen chronological paired simulation

Protocol benchmarks/adaptive_trust_protocol.json, predeclared seed **20261015**; **6 regimes x 160 = 960** wholly synthetic decision cases with a separate chronological audit stream and **5 policies**. The simulated false-assertion penalty is 4 normalized utility units. Future potential answers are not visible to KEP. All policies receive the same cases, fictional true labels, source messages, allowed options, deadlines, rights, independent audit stream and possible probe outcomes. This is not retraining on a shared holdout.

| Regime | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| stable | Source-error rates remain matched to their original assumptions |
| abrupt_degradation | Root and probe quality deteriorates abruptly after step 60 |
| gradual_degradation | Sensor quality worsens progressively |
| late_adjudication | Independent labels arrive after a 24-hour delay rather than 3 hours |
| audit_outage | New labels never arrive after step 55; old history eventually ages out |
| correlated_failure | Different roots can produce falsely agreeing claims from one bad rumor |

Compared policies: NEVER, fixed CORE-002 VOI, CHEAPEST, rolling posterior-mean VOI, conservative lower-bound gate. Each scenario has independent historical background audits over at least 72 hours before its query. Background adjudications for future times are generated once, then **filtered by each query cutoff before any policy is called**. Audit IDs are not test-case IDs.

Metrics include correct/false synthetic assertions, source checks, cost, missed truths, normalized utility, fraction of decisions with inadequate or uncertain past audits and paired bootstrap differences. Bootstrap uncertainty is conditional on this invented distribution. A conservative method may lose true opportunities, and there is no predetermined claim it must win. An observed positive root, even combined with an independent one, means source agreement only, never proof of a physical business.

    python -m benchmarks.adaptive_trust_eval
    python -m unittest discover -s tests -v

GitHub CI on Python 3.11 and 3.13 must pass the existing historical/offline replays AND the new completely synthetic trial, then preserve only aggregated results. The first successful exact aggregate is pinned by SHA-256 after checking both runtimes; any later change to results must be investigated, not silently blessed.

## What is NOT established

No real provider was audited or calibrated. Real human field reviews = 0. No source downloads, no monetary API cost, no real-world true/false venue claims, no KEP or Laya/MiMo deployed model assessment, no proof that publisher roots are independent, and no production publish rights. The 0.62 quality threshold and 72-hour window are **simulator design choices**. They are not production trust settings. Independent synthetic audit history is provided exogenously and incurs no simulated review labor cost; reported check costs exclude that fictional audit infrastructure. A later simulator must account for label acquisition costs, censoring and selection bias if dynamic methods appear promising.

## Rollback

Revert CORE-003 adaptive_trust module, the synthetic chronological generator, its tests/config, offline replay/CI addition, this document and ADR-0027. Keep CORE-001, CORE-002, every pinned historical source snapshot, all old test artifacts and FIELD-001 private research files intact. No database, Web Push, production or user-facing migration to undo.

### Mistakes, omissions and tradeoffs recorded rather than hidden

- In the abrupt degradation case, frozen VOI ran **92** simulated checks, made **5 false assertions** among **73** assertions. Adaptive posterior-mean checked **73**, with **2 false assertions** among **68** and increased fictional utility to 0.320250. The conservative method made 2 mistakes out of 53 assertions, but lost coverage and utility, scoring 0.232719. Reduced mistakes do NOT automatically maximize the chosen utility.
- Under gradual degradation, conservative gated **48** new checks, with **2 false assertions** among 40 total; fixed VOI checked 81, with 6 false out of 61. The posterior-mean strategy was actually worse than fixed VOI on this regime (0.118969 versus 0.135531).
- During audit outage, the conservative method proposed just **63** checks versus **107** by fixed VOI and made **0** false assertions, but its 0.367219 utility was much WORSE than 0.585594 because it **missed 57 simulated true opportunities** versus 17 for the original fixed policy. The uncertainty gate must not be called a universal improvement.
- In the correlated-failure scenario, all three methods scored **identically** and each made **3 false assertions**. This shows the proposed learning algorithm does not detect source-root dependence when each individual publisher looks reliable on its own independent background audits.
- Stable case: all three were identical. Audit delay: fixed VOI outperformed both learners under these assumptions. No unique superior policy was established.
- The source-audit stream is independently generated and not a policy-selected set of cases, but it comes for free in this simulator: reviewer effort and sample acquisition cost are NOT part of the utility figure. Real-world selection bias and inaccessible ground truth remain unsolved.

This is a locked research artifact for regression detection, not an optimization benchmark to tune repeatedly until it favors CORE-003. If a later policy is changed, test it on a **separate newly frozen synthetic holdout**, explicitly recognizing that current results influenced the next design.
